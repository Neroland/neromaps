# Atlases and the Cartography Desk

An atlas is your explored map as an item you can carry, copy and give away. The Cartography Desk is where you copy,
merge, label and print atlases.

## Recipes

| Item | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Blank Atlas | A book in the centre, paper on the four sides, iron nuggets in the corners |
| Atlas Page Pack | Shapeless: four paper and an iron nugget |
| Atlas Binding Ring | Iron ingots on the four sides, iron nuggets in the corners, centre empty |
| Cartography Desk | Paper, glass pane, paper on top; iron ingot, cartography table, iron ingot in the middle; three iron ingots below |

Each recipe unlocks in the recipe book when you pick up one of its key ingredients (for the desk, a cartography
table).

## Atlas

Use a **Blank Atlas** and it records your explored map of the dimension you are in, nearest regions first, up to its
capacity: 16 regions of 32 x 32 chunks, plus 16 per **Page Pack**. An atlas takes up to three Page Packs, or eight
once it has a **Binding Ring**. Fit an upgrade by using it with the atlas in your other hand.

- **Use** a filled atlas to merge what it holds into your own map. You now see that ground on every map, including the
  app.
- **Sneak-use** it to record your current map into it again.
- **Give it to a friend** and they can merge it: that is how you share a map physically, with no request and no
  ongoing link.

An atlas holds which chunks were explored, its dimension, its upgrades and its label. It never holds who recorded it,
and it holds no picture of the terrain: a merged area is drawn from current terrain, so the land looks the way it is
now.

## Cartography Desk

Open the desk with **use**. Its tabs:

| Tab | Put in | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Copy | a filled atlas (kept) and a blank atlas (used) | a copy, with the blank's capacity |
| Merge | two filled atlases of one dimension (both used) | one atlas with both areas |
| Label | an atlas, and type a label | the labelled atlas (labels pass the same name filter as waypoints) |
| Print | a filled atlas (kept) and paper | a printed Wall Map of the atlas's area |
| Print | a sign | a Signpost |

On the Print tab, the button under the slots sets how many blocks each wall-map panel covers (32, 64 or 128). The
preview shows the atlas's coverage. The desk keeps nothing: whatever is in its slots comes back to you when you close
it or break the desk.

What to do with a printed map and a signpost: [Wall Maps and Signposts](Wall-Maps-and-Signposts).

## Not in 0.1.0

- There is no page-by-page reading view for an atlas: you use, merge and copy it.
- The Survey Kit is not part of 0.1.0.
